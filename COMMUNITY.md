# Community

## Welcome

Welcome to **neuron-wire** — a research project exploring decentralized neural computation over P2P networks.

This is **not a product**. It is a research artifact — an evolving experiment in how far we can push distributed, neuroplastic learning runtimes. The goal is to produce reproducible, publishable findings about P2P neural computation, not to deliver a polished end-user application.

Whether you're a researcher, a Rust enthusiast, a distributed-systems hacker, or someone curious about decentralized AGI: you are welcome here. Bring your curiosity, your hypotheses, and your willingness to engage in evidence-based discussion.

---

## Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). In short:
- Be respectful and constructive
- Prioritize evidence over opinion
- Assume good faith
- Harassment, personal attacks, and promotion of unrelated products will not be tolerated

All community spaces — issues, discussions, Discord, and pull requests — fall under this code.

## How to Contribute

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full contributor guide. Highlights:

- **Code changes:** follow existing style, add tests, maintain zero-warnings policy
- **Experiments:** include raw data, exact commands, and seeds
- **Documentation:** write for readers unfamiliar with the field
- **Negative results are welcome:** disproving a hypothesis is a valuable contribution

---

## Community Expectations

neuron-wire is a research-first project. The community norms reflect that:

1. **Evidence-based discussion.** Claims should be supported by data, theory, or reproducible experiments. "I think" is fine; "I tested and found" is better.
2. **Respectful debate.** Disagreement is expected and healthy in research. Challenge ideas, not people. Assume the other person is acting in good faith.
3. **Constructive feedback.** When critiquing, suggest alternatives. When praising, be specific. "This is wrong" is less useful than "This claim is not supported by the data because…"
4. **Inclusivity.** This project welcomes contributors regardless of background, experience level, or identity. We are all learning.
5. **Focus.** Keep discussion related to neuron-wire and its research goals. Off-topic promotion, recruiting, or product advertising is not appropriate.
